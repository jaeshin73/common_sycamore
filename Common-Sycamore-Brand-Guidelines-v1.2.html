01

Why it looks this way

Common Sycamore cultivates courage for Asian Americans to define and pursue their own paths. Three ideas from that mission shape every decision in this system.

Distinct, together

Three forms, each with its own shape and color, become one mark. Every part of the system keeps its character and still clearly belongs.

Rooted and growing

Shapes grow from the place where they meet. Layouts build steadily on a 4px base, and motion is calm and directional, never decorative.

Room for every story

Generous space, plain words and real people. The system steps back so the people Common Sycamore serves can come forward.

02

Logo

Three distinct forms come together as one mark. None copies another, and none is complete alone. The thin spaces between them matter as much as the shapes: they are the in-between spaces of relationship, treated as an asset.

Tagline lockupNew in 1.1

The with-tagline version is for moments that need a short line of support. Use the version without a tagline when space is tight or the line already appears nearby. Set the tagline in Fraunces Regular directly below the logo, left-aligned with the wordmark (the logotype), not the mark. It is sized at about 3.4% of the logo width, with letter-spacing opened about 1.6%, so it starts and ends exactly on the wordmark’s left and right edges. The gap to the logo is X. Minimum size

The tagline never goes below 14px on screen or 9.5pt in print, because Fraunces turns thin and hard to read when it is smaller. That sets the smallest logo: Standard + tagline 410px wide (103mm in print) and Stacked + tagline 270px wide (68mm in print). Below that, switch to the version without a tagline, which goes down to 140px and 96px.

Usage

Use the tagline “Cultivating courage for Asian Americans” as written. Never resize it on its own, set it in Plex Sans or bold, or place it beside the mark.

Clear space

X equals half the height of a leaf. Keep X clear on every side, and keep the gap between mark and wordmark at X. Scale X with the logo.

Color versions

Full color on white and light neutrals. Grayscale where color printing is unavailable. On photos and dark backgrounds, use grayscale on black so each shape keeps its own tone.

Don’ts

Keep the logo exactly as supplied.

Don’t recolor the shapes or swap their colors.

Don’t rotate, rearrange or separate the shapes.

Don’t stretch the logo or add shadows and effects.

Don’t retype the wordmark or set it in another font.

Don’t place the full-color logo on photos. Use grayscale on black.

03

Color

Three primaries carry the brand. Warm neutrals do most of the everyday work. Every color is a named token in Figma and in code, so the palette can evolve without touching every screen.

Warm neutrals

Built from warm greys so the page never feels clinical. Use them for surfaces, borders, secondary text and disabled states.

Tonal scales

Each primary sits at 500. Lighter steps tint backgrounds and hover fills; darker steps handle pressed states and accessible text.

Print valuesNew in 1.1

RGB is for screens. CMYK is for ordinary printing, converted from sRGB for coated paper (FOGRA39). Sycamore Blue is more vivid than CMYK can print, so it looks deeper on paper. Proof before a large run.

Pantone covers four colors: the three primaries and Ink.New in 1.2

Use Pantone for spot-color printing such as merchandise, signage and packaging. Ink, the color of the wordmark and tagline, now has its own spot ink, so the full-color logo prints in four Pantone colors.

  • Sycamore Blue · half circlePANTONE Blue 072 C
  • Sunlight · leafPANTONE 7405 C
  • Bloom · leafPANTONE 245 C
  • Ink · wordmark and taglinePANTONE Neutral Black C

These are the nearest Solid Coated matches by calculation. Blue 072 C is deeper than Sycamore Blue, because no Pantone ink matches it closely. Neutral Black C is a warm, soft black that sits close to Ink. Confirm every match against a physical swatch book before printing.

Accessibility

Every pairing is checked against WCAG 2.1 AA: at least 4.5:1 for body text, and 3:1 for large text and UI shapes.

Blue works as text and as a fill.

White on Blue 500 reaches 8.3:1, well past the 4.5:1 AA minimum for body text.

Yellow and Pink are fills only.

On white they reach only 1.5:1 and 1.8:1. Put Ink (#1C1917) text on them instead: 11.7:1 and 9.6:1.

Secondary text is Neutral 700.

Neutral 700 (#3F3A37) reaches 11.2:1 on white. Neutral 400 (#89807A) is for disabled text only.

Never color alone.

Status always pairs a color with an icon or a label, so it reads in grayscale and for colorblind readers.

Contrast check

Each brand color with White and Ink (#1C1917) text, plus brand-colored text on White and Ink, measured live. AA needs 4.5:1 for body text; AA Large needs 3:1 for text 24px and up, or 19px bold.

Typography

Two families, both free on Google Fonts. IBM Plex Sans carries information: body, interface and headings. Fraunces gives the brand its voice in short, human moments. Tenon stays in the logo only, so the wordmark always stands apart.

06

Layout

Everything sits on a 4px base and an 8px rhythm. The more something acts or represents people, the rounder it gets.

Spacing

Space grows with how unrelated two things are: 4–16 inside a component, 16–32 between elements, 40–64 between groups, 96–120 between sections.

Grid

Content never runs wider than 1280px. On wider screens the grid stays centered.
